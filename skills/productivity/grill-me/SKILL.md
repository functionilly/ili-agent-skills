---
name: grill-me
description: Interview the user relentlessly about a plan or design until shared understanding, one branch of the decision tree at a time. Use when the user wants to stress-test a plan or says "grill me".
---

Interview me about every part of this plan until we reach a shared understanding.
Walk down each branch of the design tree. Resolve dependencies between decisions one by
one. Give your recommended answer with each question.

Ask one question at a time.

If the codebase can answer a question, explore the codebase instead.
